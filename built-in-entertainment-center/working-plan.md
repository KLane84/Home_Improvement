## Concept (short)

Permanently encase three base units (65" center console + two 27" Lowe's cabinets) in **¾" plywood**, with **3½″ ladder risers under the side cabinets only** (2×4 on edge), **stepped counters** (sides higher than center), and **27" × 12" shelving towers** to ceiling trim — all painted **Benjamin Moore White Dove** (semi or high gloss). TV stays wall-mounted as today. Relocate power up behind the TV; limited in-cabinet tech (DVD/Blu-ray). **Path A:** centered 119″ group, ~20⅛″ open painted flanks, tight scribe + continuous face skin between units.

## Locked design decisions

| Decision | Choice | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Layout path | **Path A** | Centered carcasses; open outer flanks |
| Between-unit joints | Scribe + face skin | No 6″ void fillers |
| Flanks | ~**20⅛″** each open | (159¼ − 119) ÷ 2; painted wall, not beadboard |
| Encasement | Permanent plywood skin | Units do not slide out |
| Access | Through cabinet interiors | Power at/near cabinet tops |
| Countertop | **Stepped** — sides higher than center | Not a continuous flush full-span blank |
| Risers | **3½″ on side cabinets only** | 2×4 on edge; center height unchanged |
| Side counter top AFF | **34¼″** | 3½ + 30 + ¾ |
| Step (sides vs center) | **~4¼″** | Before any center cap |
| Finish | Benjamin Moore White Dove | Semi or high gloss TBD |
| Uppers | Open shelving towers ×2 | 27" W × 12" D to ceiling trim |
| Beadboard | Upper center bay only | Behind unit / between towers — not on open flanks or door faces |
| TV | Leave wall-mounted as today | No surround / aperture buildout |
| Labor | Fully DIY | Budget-friendly materials |
| Side cabinets cost | ~$75 used | Center console already owned |
| Left seating | White cushiony accent chair | Shorter than recliner; pull forward of left cabinets |
