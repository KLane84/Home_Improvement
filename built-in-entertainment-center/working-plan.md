## Concept (short)

Permanently encase three base units (65" center console + two 27" Lowe's cabinets) in **¾" plywood**, with **4" ladder risers under the side cabinets only**, **stepped counters** (sides higher than center), and **27" × 12" shelving towers** to ceiling trim — all painted **Benjamin Moore White Dove** (semi or high gloss). TV stays wall-mounted as today. Relocate power up behind the TV; limited in-cabinet tech (DVD/Blu-ray). Flank / gap layout chosen after tape mock-up of Paths A / B / C.

## Locked design decisions

| Decision | Choice | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Encasement | Permanent plywood skin | Units do not slide out |
| Access | Through cabinet interiors | Power at/near cabinet tops |
| Countertop | **Stepped** — sides higher than center | Not a continuous flush full-span blank |
| Risers | **4" on side cabinets only** | Center console height unchanged |
| Finish | Benjamin Moore White Dove | Semi or high gloss TBD |
| Uppers | Open shelving towers ×2 | 27" W × 12" D to ceiling trim |
| TV | Leave wall-mounted as today | No surround / aperture buildout |
| Labor | Fully DIY | Budget-friendly materials |
| Side cabinets cost | ~$75 used | Center console already owned |

## Open design forks

Evaluate and pick from [dead-space-options.md](./dead-space-options.md):
