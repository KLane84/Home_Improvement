Two open towers above the side cabinets: **27″ W × 12″ D**, height to crown. Shelves and carcass from **¾″ plywood**.

## Height formula (do this on site)

```
side_counter_AFF = 3½″ (riser) + 30″ (cabinet) + ¾″ (counter) = 34¼″

tower_height = (floor_to_underside_of_crown_at_tower) − 34¼″
```

**Placeholder** (96″ FFE, ~3½″ crown → underside 92½″):

```
tower_height ≈ 92½ − 34¼ = 58¼″
```

Cut lists below use **H = 58¼″**. Recalculate before cutting sides.

## Carcass joinery (simple box)

- Two **sides** full height × 12″ deep  
- **Top** and **bottom** fit **between** sides → width **27″ − ¾″ − ¾″ = 25½″**  
- Fixed or adjustable **shelves** same as top/bottom: **25½″ × 12″ × ¾″**  
- Shelf count (**locked for Path A cut list**): **4 intermediate shelves** + bottom + top → **5** open bays. (Do not cut three-mid variants unless you revise this guide and nesting.)

## Nesting on 4×8 sheets (¾″)

**Sheet A — sides**

```
48″ height × 96″ length
Four 12″×58¼″ sides: place as 12″ strips along the 96″ length.
58¼ < 96 → each side is one piece from a 12″-wide strip.
Four sides need 48″ of sheet width exactly (4×12) × 58¼″ length.
→ One full sheet almost consumed by sides alone (48×58¼ used).
```

**Sheet B — horizontals (25½ × 12)**

```
12 pieces × 25½″ × 12″
Along 96″: 25½ × 3 = 76½″ per row (3 per row) → 4 rows = 12 pieces
Rows need 12×4 = 48″ width → exactly one sheet.
```

**Buy (towers only):** **2** sheets of ¾″ × 4×8 (plus kerf waste / mistakes — consider **+1** sheet shared with counters).

**With counters (02):** counters are two 27×16 — fit on a third sheet or leftover if you buy a third for encasement later. Practical buy for counters + towers: **3** sheets of ¾″ (sides sheet, horizontals sheet, counters + scrap / face skins).

## Build steps

1. Measure crown underside AFF at left and right tower locations; compute **H**.
2. Cut sides to **H**; cut all 25½ × 12 horizontals.
3. Dry-assemble; mark shelf spacing (~equal clear openings).
4. Glue/screw box; square; attach bottom to counter or cleats.
5. Fasten tower to wall studs (anti-tip) through side or French cleat.
6. Edge-band, fill, prime, White Dove.
7. Scribe top to crown; shoe molding if needed.

## Hardware (optional)

- Shelf pins for adjustable shelves (then skip dadoes; shelves still 25½ × 12)
- Pocket screws or confirmat screws at carcass joints
