# 01 — Side risers (2×4 ladder frames)

Two identical platforms under the **27″ × 15″** side cabinets. Center console: **no riser**.

## Spec

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Qty | **2** (left + right) |
| Outer footprint | **27″ W × 15″ D × 3½″ H** |
| Material | Kiln-dried **2×4** (actual **1½″ × 3½″**) |
| Orientation | **On edge** — the **3½″** face is vertical height |
| Joinery | Butt joints + pocket screws or glue + screws; optional pocket from inside |

## Assembly (plan view)

```
        ← 27″ overall →
   ┌─────────────────────┐  ↑
   │████████ front rail █│  │
   │█                 █│  15″ overall
   │█  stretcher(s)   █│  │
   │█                 █│  │
   │████████ back rail ██│  ↓
   └─────────────────────┘
     sides sit BETWEEN front/back
```

- **Front & back rails:** full **27″** length (show 1½″ face in plan).
- **Side rails:** length **15″ − 1½″ − 1½″ = 12″** (fit between front and back).
- **Mid stretchers:** same as sides (**12″**) — two per riser under the cabinet for support.

## Nesting from 8′ (96″) 2×4s

| Board | Cuts | Used | Leftover |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2×4 #1 | 27″ + 27″ + 27″ | 81″ | ~15″ scrap |
| 2×4 #2 | 27″ + 12″ + 12″ + 12″ + 12″ | 75″ | ~21″ scrap |
| 2×4 #3 | 12″ × 4 | 48″ | ~48″ (keep for cleats / mistakes) |

**Buy:** **3** pieces of 8′ 2×4 (or **2** if you accept tighter nesting and less spare).

## Build steps

1. Cut all parts; label L/R riser sets.
2. Dry-fit rectangle: outer 27 × 15; confirm height **3½″**.
3. Assemble front/back/sides square; add mid stretchers.
4. Optional: ¾″ plywood skin on top of riser if you want a continuous deck under the cabinet (not required if cabinet sits on rails — then add **R5** top deck **27″ × 15″ × ¾″** per riser).
5. Level on floor; place cabinets; screw cabinets to risers from inside if desired.

## Optional top deck (¾″ ply)

If you want a solid platform instead of sitting on 2×4 edges:

| ID | Piece | Qty (both risers) | Size |
|---|---|---|---|
| R5 | Top deck | 2 | **27″ × 15″ × ¾″** |

Adds **¾″** to stack height → side counter AFF becomes **35″** unless you notch or skip decks. Prefer **no deck** to keep **34¼″** side top unless you like the solid feel.
