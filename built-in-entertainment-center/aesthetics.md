# Aesthetics & Design Intent

## Design direction (one sentence)

Paint-grade built-in: existing furniture encased in plywood (with full-span countertop and upper shelving towers) so the wall reads as one continuous millwork unit, finished in **Benjamin Moore White Dove**.

## Construction look

- **Encasement:** All three base units wrapped/faced in plywood for the built-in silhouette
- **Countertop:** Continuous across the full composite span (~131")
- **Uppers:** Built-in shelving towers above side cabinets (27" × 12" × to ceiling trim)
- **Unified finish:** Surround painted **Benjamin Moore White Dove**
- **Sheen:** Likely **semi-gloss or high-gloss** (confirm on sample board)
- **Existing trim:** Keep / match **white** to existing crown & base; White Dove may be close enough — sample before committing to repaint all room trim

## Visual hierarchy

1. Primary focus: Unified White Dove millwork wall (built-in architecture)
2. Secondary focus: TV as centered aperture; open shelves on the towers
3. Quiet / recessed: Encased carcasses; in-cabinet DVD/Blu-ray; cable chase

## Match to the room

| Element | Existing | Built-in approach |
|---|---|---|
| Wall color | Light grey | White Dove built-in (sample against wall) |
| Trim profile | White crown + white baseboard | New trim white to match existing; White Dove OK if close |
| Floor / base | Dark wood-look plank | 4" ladder-frame risers + toe-kick / base integration |
| Adjacent room | Floral wallpaper + white wainscot | No requirement to match |
