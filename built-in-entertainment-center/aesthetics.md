# Aesthetics & Design Intent

## Design direction (one sentence)

Paint-grade built-in: existing furniture encased in plywood (with **stepped** side counters higher than the center, and upper shelving towers) so the wall reads as coordinated millwork, finished in **Benjamin Moore White Dove**.

## Construction look

- **Layout:** **Path A** — centered three-unit group, open painted flanks (~20⅛″ each), continuous face skin
- **Encasement:** All three base units wrapped/faced in plywood for the built-in silhouette
- **Countertop:** **Stepped** — side cabinets (on **3½″** risers) get higher counters; center console stays lower
- **Uppers:** Built-in shelving towers above side cabinets (27" × 12" × to ceiling trim)
- **TV:** Remains wall-mounted as today — not a framed aperture / surround project
- **Unified finish:** Surround painted **Benjamin Moore White Dove**
- **Sheen:** Likely **semi-gloss or high-gloss** (confirm on sample board)
- **Existing trim:** Keep / match **white** to existing crown & base; White Dove may be close enough — sample before committing to repaint all room trim

## Visual hierarchy

1. Primary focus: Unified White Dove millwork (built-in architecture), with a clear **height step** at the sides
2. Secondary focus: TV as the existing wall-mounted centerpiece; open shelves on the towers
3. Quiet / recessed: Encased carcasses; in-cabinet DVD/Blu-ray; cable chase; open Path A flanks (painted wall)

## Match to the room

| Element | Existing | Built-in approach |
|---|---|---|
| Wall color | Light grey | White Dove built-in (sample against wall) |
| Trim profile | White crown + white baseboard | New trim white to match existing; White Dove OK if close |
| Floor / base | Dark wood-look plank | **3½″** ladder-frame risers under **sides only** + toe-kick / base integration |
| Adjacent room | Floral wallpaper + white wainscot | No requirement to match |

## Materials & finish

- Structure: 2×4 framing / ladder risers under side cabinets (**on edge → 3½″**)
- Face / panels: **¾" plywood** encasement + Path A end returns / face skin
- Accent / edges: **1×2** and decorative trim (profiles TBD)
- Countertop: Stepped blanks — side **¾″** ply; center painted/capped to match as needed
- Paint: **Benjamin Moore White Dove** on **all** millwork and **all three** base units (center console painted to match — not left dark)
- Hardware: TBD (center has brass pulls today; sides have knobs)
- Lighting: undecided (leave provision)

## Do / don’t

- **Do:** Encases furniture in plywood; stepped side counters; White Dove on new work
- **Do:** Built-in shelves above side cabinets to ceiling trim
- **Do:** Leave TV mount / position as-is unless electrical forces a small move
- **Do:** White vertical **beadboard** as upper wall backing in the **center bay only** (Path A)
- **Do:** Shorter white cushiony accent chair forward of left cabinets (replace recliner)
- **Don’t:** Leave dark center console visually exposed as a contrasting island
- **Don’t:** Put beadboard on open Path A flanks or on lower cabinet door faces
- **Don’t:** Assume one continuous flush countertop across all three units
- **Don’t:** Use legacy 14⅛″ / 131″ flank sketches — Path A is **~20⅛″** / **119″**
