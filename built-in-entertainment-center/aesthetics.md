# Aesthetics & Design Intent

## Design direction (one sentence)

Paint-grade built-in: existing furniture encased in plywood so the wall reads as one continuous millwork unit, finished in **White Dove**.

## Construction look

- **Encasement:** All three units (dark center console + two white flanking cabinets) wrapped/faced in plywood to create the built-in look
- **Unified finish:** Entire surround (and visible encased faces as decided) painted **White Dove**
- **Paint brand / product line:** TBD — follow-up action to confirm (commonly Benjamin Moore OC-17; verify sheen and exact SKU before buying)

## Visual hierarchy

1. Primary focus: Unified White Dove millwork wall (built-in architecture)
2. Secondary focus: TV as centered aperture within the surround
3. Should stay quiet / recessed: Existing furniture carcasses (concealed by plywood); cables; soundbar integration TBD

## Match to the room

| Element | Existing | Built-in approach |
|---|---|---|
| Wall color | Light grey | Built-in in White Dove (contrast or soft complement — confirm against wall sample) |
| Trim profile | White crown + white baseboard | Meet / return into existing trim; paint strategy for new vs. existing trim TBD |
| Floor / base | Dark wood-look plank | New toe-kick / base integration with plywood encasement |
| Other millwork nearby | White wainscot in adjacent room | Optional harmony with White Dove — not required |

## Do / don’t

- Do: Encases furniture in plywood for a true built-in silhouette
- Do: Paint the assembled surround White Dove for a single finished color
- Don’t: Leave the dark center console as a contrasting “furniture island” inside the new surround (unless revised later)
- Don’t: Buy paint until brand + sheen are confirmed
