| Riser | L | W | H | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Central | — | — | **0"** | No riser |

**Stack heights (sides, AFF):**
- Riser **3½″** + cabinet **30″** + counter **¾″** = **34¼″** side counter top
- Center ~**30″** (no riser)
- **Step** ≈ **4¼″**

Do **not** add optional riser top decks unless you intentionally accept a taller side stack (see riser guide).
